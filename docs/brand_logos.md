# Brand logos (TIR Bazarı "Marka" page)

Owner decision (25.10.2026): only original logos whose **source and terms of
use were checked** are packaged. Every other brand keeps its letter square
(`brandInitials`). No logo is drawn, traced or guessed.

How a logo is added:

1. Source: the brand's official press / media kit, or a Wikimedia Commons
   file page whose licence tag allows reuse (e.g. `PD-textlogo`). Files
   tagged non-free / fair use are not used.
2. The file is only scaled down (no colour or shape change), saved as PNG
   with a transparent background, 128 × 128 px or smaller, under
   `assets/images/brands/<slug>.png`.
3. It is listed in `kMarketBrandLogos`
   (`lib/features/market/domain/market_brand_logos.dart`) and in the table
   below with its source page and licence.

Status 25.10.2026 (Mərhələ 3): 20 logos from Simple Icons
(raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons, `data/simple-icons.json`
for source, colour, guidelines and licence). Simple Icons' drawing data is
CC0, but the marks stay trademarks of their owners and Simple Icons asks
users to seek permission themselves (its DISCLAIMER.md). The brands' own
sites could not be opened from the build environment, so their terms were
not read; the owner decides on publishing (nominative use: the logo only
names the brand of an ad). Second set (owner, 25.10.2026): 24 logos from the open car-logos-dataset
(raw.githubusercontent.com, images from carlogos.org, original colours,
only trimmed and scaled; Peterbilt without its "CLASS PAYS" slogan,
Mercedes-Benz as its star emblem alone — the wordmark was unreadable at 44 pt) and
the Volvo iron mark (it is the mark of Volvo Trucks as well). Not used:
Howo (only the Sinotruk group mark was found), Ford Trucks, Mitsubishi
Fuso, Tata Daewoo, Ashok Leyland (only the parent brand's mark). Krone,
Kögel and Schmitz Cargobull: from a picture the owner supplied. The other
88 brands (the other trailers, all construction machines, Ural, KrAZ, BMC,
Sitrak …) were not in any reachable source — they keep the letter square.
