TırLink

TırLink is a web platform that brings cargo owners, carriers, services and sellers together. It runs in the browser at tirlink.app and can be added to a phone’s home screen like an app.

What the platform offers

  • Loads — freight listings, searchable by route (from, to) and type of load.
  • Carriers — empty truck listings: where the truck is now, where it is heading and how much free capacity it has.
  • Truck Market — listings for buying and selling trucks, trailers, semi-trailers and other vehicles.
  • Map — truck parks, service centres and spare parts shops on a map; users can add truck parks, services and shops.
  • My trucks — a section where a carrier keeps track of their own trucks, jobs and expenses.
  • Job Center — listings for drivers looking for work and for employers looking for drivers.
  • Messages — conversations between users inside the platform.

How to use it

  • Loads, carriers, the Truck Market and the map can be viewed without an account.
  • Posting listings, messaging and using the Job Center require signing in (with e-mail or Google).
  • Languages: Azerbaijani, Turkish, English, Russian, Georgian and Chinese.
